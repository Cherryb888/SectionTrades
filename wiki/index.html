Smart money & flows

Read this before using any of these as a signal. Following one whale loses money — you're always late, you don't see their hedges, and survivorship bias flatters the ones you've heard of. These sources are useful for aggregate positioning (are large holders net adding or distributing?), flow changes (is the direction shifting?), and labeled on-chain moves (exchange deposits ≈ likely selling). Treat them as macro inputs with a days-to-weeks lag. Do not size a scalp off a whale tweet.

Crypto on-chain & exchange positioning

Lookonchain

Free on-chain whale tracking with entity labels. Regularly flags smart-money wallets and large exchange deposits/withdrawals. Report-style posts, not hype.

  • lookonchain.com
  • X: @lookonchain

Arkham Intelligence

Entity-labeled on-chain. See where named funds, exchanges, treasuries, whales are moving assets. Free tier is usable.

  • arkhamintelligence.com

CoinGlass

Aggregate derivatives: long/short ratios across exchanges, open interest, liquidation heatmaps, funding rates. Best single source for "is the market crowded one way."

  • Long/short ratios
  • Liquidation heatmap

Glassnode

On-chain analytics — holder cohort behavior, realized price, MVRV. Free tier limited but still useful. Paid for depth.

  • studio.glassnode.com

Whale Alert

Real-time feed of large transactions. Noisier than Lookonchain — most alerts are internal exchange shuffles, not signal. Use with scepticism.

  • whale-alert.io

Farside / SoSoValue — ETF flows

Daily net flows into spot BTC ETFs (IBIT, FBTC, etc.). The single most tradeable institutional-flow number for BTC.

  • Farside — BTC ETF flows
  • SoSoValue — spot BTC ETFs

Company insiders buying/selling. Insider selling is noisy (options vesting, diversification). Insider buying with own cash is a higher-quality signal.

Review triggers: interim at 20 trades, meaningful stats at 30–50. No rule changes before n=30.

Session checklist (use this each sit-down)

  1. State check — slept 6h+, eaten, not tilted, no carry-over anger from last session. If any fail: close platform.
  2. Levels — mark prior-day high/low, session VWAP, key levels from the instrument card above.
  3. Catalyst scan — any scheduled events in this window? Avoid first 90s after prints.
  4. Setup hunt — only ORB / VWAP reclaim-reject / failed breakout / news reaction. Anything else = don't take.
  5. Pre-trade gate — entry, stop, target written; size = (£100 × 0.5%) / (entry − stop); stop order placed with position.
  6. Log at entry — journal entry created within 15 min of fill, with emotional state named honestly.
  7. Session kill rules — 2 losers → end. Daily loss −£1.50 → end. 5 trades → end.
  8. Session review — one paragraph in the journal at session close: setup quality, rule adherence, anything to flag.
